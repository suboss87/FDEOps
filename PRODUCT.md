# FDEOps product and fieldbook design

## Product purpose

Help Forward Deployed Engineers turn customer problems into working systems through skills for strategy, architecture and engineering. Use individual task skills with an AI coding agent, or `fde` to coordinate ongoing work. Local customer memory supports continuity between sessions.

## Fieldbook users and purpose

Forward Deployed Engineers and independent experts managing several client engagements open the fieldbook before a meeting, after an interruption or during handover, on laptops and phones.

The fieldbook makes next actions, risks, evidence and recorded acceptance easy to inspect. It is a generated read-only view; the local Markdown record remains authoritative. Continue work through the skills or local CLI.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, practical. Preserve the existing FDEOps identity and make routine delivery work easier to read.

## Anti-references

Decorative AI chat shells, promotional metric cards, fabricated live status, and controls that pretend to edit a static report.

## Design Principles

- Show what needs attention and why, without equating a missing record with a failed client engagement.
- Keep client selection obvious; preserve separate records and explicit acceptance.
- Make overview filters and navigation agree.
- Explain when the snapshot was generated and how to continue the work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, and narrow screens. Status must have text as well as color. Evidence tables may scroll inside their own region.
